Carroll Football Analytics

Dashboards built from the program's own tracked data — special teams, strength testing, and (eventually) offense/defense. Replaces the old Tableau Public dashboards with something that lives alongside the rest of the data pipeline.

Phase 1 · Unit-Level Overview

Special Teams Overview Live

Money Unit, Punt, Punt Return, Kickoff, and Kickoff Return — modernized from the original Tableau dashboard, same metrics and charts. Two metrics (Snap Rating, exact miss-location) aren't in the source data yet and are flagged rather than faked.

Lifting & Strength Live

All Time / Last Session, Senior/Junior, Sophomore/Freshman leaderboards, and athlete-vs-athlete Class Comparison — rebuilt from the Lifting Data pipeline's Combined Total/Bench/Squat/Clean.

Phase 2 · Position-Level (Special Teams)

Placekicker Live

PAT/FG individual breakdown — Executive Scorecard, Kicker Head-to-Head, and Situational Deep Dive, complementing the Money Unit tab with per-kicker splits.

Kickoff Kicker Live

Touchback/Inside-25 rate, distance, return allowed, onside recovery — Executive Scorecard, Kicker Head-to-Head, and Situational Deep Dive.

Punter Live

Gross/net distance, Inside-20 rate, touchback rate, outcome mix — Executive Scorecard, Punter Head-to-Head, and Situational Deep Dive.

Short Snapper Planned

PAT/FG snap accuracy and timing.

Long Snapper Planned

Punt snap accuracy and timing.
