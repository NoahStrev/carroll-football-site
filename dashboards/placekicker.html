Placekicker

Individual placekicker breakdown from the real Carroll PAT-FG sheet in Carroll_Special_Teams_2021_Current.xlsx (236 kicks, 2021–2025, this coach's tenure) — complements the unit-level Money Unit tab on Special Teams Overview with per-kicker splits. Snap to Catch, Catch to Kick, Hash Kicked From, and Snap Location are hand-charted from film by the coaching staff and only exist from 2023 onward (0% charted 2021–2022, effectively complete 2024–2025) — every chart using them only ever averages the rows that actually have a value, never padded with blanks for the uncharted years. Snap Location is a snap-quality ranking the coaching staff is charting (scale may be 1-3 or 1-5, still being finalized) — which end means "better" isn't confirmed yet, so it's shown as whatever raw buckets exist rather than relabeled "good/bad". Miss Location only distinguishes "Blocked" from everything else — the source never records a wide-left/wide-right/short direction, so that breakdown isn't shown here rather than invented. PAT/FG Value and Score are a real, fully-computed "points added over expectation" metric already in the source workbook (see that project's rules/carroll_pat_fg_rules.md) — 0 (average) is the baseline, positive is better than expected for the distance attempted, out of a 0–100 rescale for Score.
