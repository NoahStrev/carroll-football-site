Kickoff Kicker

Individual kickoff-kicker breakdown from the real Carroll Kickoff sheet in Carroll_Special_Teams_2021_Current.xlsx (262 kickoffs, 2021–2025) — position 2 of the special-teams series, alongside Placekicker. Touchback rate, Inside-25 rate, distance, return allowed, onside recovery, and quarter/season splits are all box-score-derived and fully real for every row. Hangtime and Hash Kicked From are hand-charted from film and only exist from 2023 onward (0% charted 2021–2022, effectively complete by 2024–2025) — charts using them only ever average the rows actually charted. Kickoff Value/Score is a real, fully-computed "points added over expectation" metric already in the source workbook. Known data issue, not fixed here — the source has two spellings for what looks like the same kicker, "Scofield" (6 rows, 2021) and "Sebastian Scofield" (6 rows, 2021–2022) — left as two separate entries rather than silently merged, since that's a name-parsing fix that belongs in the Special Teams Data project itself (same category as the Lifting Data project's known spelling-variant duplicates), not something to guess at here.
