Special Teams Overview

All five special-teams units, rebuilt from the real Carroll_Special_Teams_2021_Current.xlsx workbook (2021–2025). Every KPI/chart here recomputes live from the filtered rows. Two things shown on the original Tableau version aren't in the current data pipeline yet and are omitted rather than faked: Snap Rating (no such column exists in the source) and the exact L/Short miss-location breakdown (only "Blocked" is currently tracked as a miss reason). Hangtime and snap-timing fields are hand-charted by the coaching staff and are real wherever populated — filters and charts here only ever use the rows that actually have a value, never an average padded with blanks.
