Long Snapper

Individual long-snapper (punt snap) breakdown from the real Snapper Name column on the Carroll Punt sheet in Carroll_Special_Teams_2021_Current.xlsx (247 punts, 2021–2025) — position 4b of the special-teams series, the true "long snap" (~13-15 yards), distinct from the FG/PAT "short snap" covered on the Short Snapper page. Blocked rate, Punter Tackle rate, net distance, outcome mix, and quarter/season splits are all box-score-derived and fully real. Only 122 of 247 punts have a snapper credited (mostly 2023 onward) — excluded from snapper-specific views, though other KPIs use every punt. Only one snapper, Brayden Partlow, is credited so far — the Head-to-Head comparison UI is still built at full fidelity (same as every other position page), since more punts will get a snapper credited over time and it should already work once a second name shows up. Snap to Catch (snap time) and Snap Location are hand-charted from film and only exist from 2023 onward — every chart using them only ever averages the rows actually charted. Snap Location is a snap-quality ranking the coaching staff is charting (scale may be 1-3 or 1-5, still being finalized) — which end means "better" isn't confirmed yet, so it's shown as whatever raw buckets exist rather than relabeled "good/bad". Punt Value/Score is a real, fully-computed metric already in the source workbook.
